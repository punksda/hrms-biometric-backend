Scheduling

Duty Roster

Roster for the selected hotel & month

Download the template — it already contains your employees and any roster saved so far — fill in Weekly Off (or WO) for off days and a shift name (or leave blank) for working days, then upload it. The Attendance Register picks it up immediately: scheduled off days show as Weekly Off, and if someone works on a Weekly Off the day is credited as a comp off. A blank cell clears that day.

Compensation

Payroll

Gross pay = Gross Salary ÷ days in the month × payable days. PF and ESI are calculated on Fixed Basic Salary, only for employees flagged applicable — PF rounds to the nearest rupee, ESI always rounds up. Professional Tax follows the current West Bengal slab. CTC = Gross Pay + Employer PF + Employer ESI. Arrear, other deductions and advance recovery are editable per employee per month.
